How to Find the Exact Middle Ground

Coordinating a meetup for a coffee, business lunch, or casual date often results in one party commuting significantly longer than the other. The Fair Halfway Meeting Point tool eliminates the guesswork by mathematically calculating the exact geographical center between two points.

Zero-Server URL Sharing

When you generate an invitation link, your latitude and longitude are temporarily attached to the URL (e.g., ?lat=40&lng=-73). We do not use databases or cloud storage. When your friend opens the link, their browser reads the URL locally and runs the calculation on their device, ensuring 100% privacy for both parties.

Midpoint Calculation Formula

The tool uses a localized geometric averaging technique combined with Leaflet map projection to pinpoint the center. Once the halfway pin drops, you can easily scan the surrounding neighborhood on the interactive map to find nearby cafes, parks, or restaurants that are equally convenient for both of you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculate driving time or straight-line distance?

This tool calculates the straight-line (Euclidean/geodesic) midpoint between the two coordinates. While driving routes can vary based on traffic, a geographical midpoint is the fairest baseline for selecting a neighborhood to meet in.

What happens if the halfway point is in the middle of a lake?

Since the math strictly finds the exact center, the pin might land on water, a highway, or a forest. Simply use the interactive map to zoom out and look for the nearest commercial area, cafe, or park adjacent to the pin.
