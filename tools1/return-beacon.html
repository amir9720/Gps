How the Return Beacon Works Offline

When you tap "Park Here", the browser queries your mobile hardware's A-GPS chip to extract latitude, longitude, and elevation. These coordinates are written directly into your browser's persistent LocalStorage sandbox. No remote server is contacted, ensuring complete anonymity and zero mobile data consumption.

Haversine Distance Formula

As you walk, your current coordinates are recalculated in real time against the saved spot using the mathematical Haversine equation. This accounts for the curvature of the Earth to produce distance measurements accurate down to single meters.

Compass Orientation & Bearing

The tool tracks forward azimuth angles by reading your smartphone's internal magnetometer sensor (DeviceOrientation). The blue pointer dynamically adjusts its angle so it always aims directly towards your parked vehicle, regardless of how you hold your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my saved spot disappear if I close the browser?

No. The location is stored inside your browser's LocalStorage memory. You can lock your phone, close Safari or Chrome, and reopen the page hours later; the arrow will instantly resume guiding you back.

Why does the compass arrow spin erratically indoors?

Large reinforced concrete structures and underground parking decks generate strong magnetic interference. For optimal accuracy, step towards an open ramp, window, or outdoor sidewalk so the magnetometer and GPS sensors can recalibrate.

Can I use this for hotels or hiking trails?

Absolutely. While optimized for finding parked vehicles, it functions as a universal waypoint beacon for hotels, trailheads, campsite tents, or festival meeting spots.
