How Browser Geofencing Protects Your Commute

The Transit Wake-Up Alarm creates an invisible digital perimeter (a geofence circle) around your target arrival stop. While you commute on public transport, the browser calculates the distance continuously using the mathematical Great-Circle Distance equation. Once your location enters the boundary radius, the alarm triggers instantly.

Synthesized Web Audio Engine

Unlike basic websites that load external MP3 audio files (which often fail to play if cellular data drops inside subway tunnels), our tool generates dynamic high-penetration sound waves locally using the browser's native Web Audio API oscillators.

Screen Wake Lock & Background Tips

Modern mobile browsers restrict background location access to preserve battery. When the alarm is activated, this tool requests a Screen Wake Lock to keep your display gently active at low power, guaranteeing uninterrupted proximity monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the alarm work underground in metro tunnels?

Direct satellite GPS signals can degrade inside deep underground tunnels. However, smartphones automatically switch to cellular tower triangulation and station Wi-Fi beacons to approximate position, snapping back to high precision as your train approaches stations.

What radius should I choose for high-speed trains vs. city buses?

For local city buses and subways, 300 to 500 meters is ideal. For regional or high-speed intercity trains travelling over 100 km/h, set the alert radius to 1,000 or 2,000 meters to give yourself adequate time to gather your belongings.

Can I silence the alarm once it starts ringing?

Yes, as soon as the alarm triggers, the main button changes to a bright red "Stop Alarm" button, allowing you to instantly silence the chime with a single tap.
